## 1. Introduction

### Takeaways

- Chess is a game for two players, one playing White and one playing Black.
- Each side has one king. Look for the cross on its crown.
- Check means the king is attacked.
- Checkmate means the king is in check and no legal response can make it safe.
- Treat the other player kindly, whether you win or lose.

Question: Find a bishop move that checkmates Black's king.

Answer explanation: Bishop to a5 gives check. The bishop controls the open escape squares, and Black's own pieces block the others. Checkmate.

### course:puzzles-mate-in-one:15

Question: Find a pawn capture that gives checkmate in this position.

Answer explanation: The pawn captures on f7, checking the king and uncovering check from the rook. No legal king move answers both attacks. Checkmate.
